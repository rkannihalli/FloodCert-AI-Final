The NFHL is the digital version of the official Flood Insurance Rate Maps (FIRMs) published by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA). This form meets the requirements of the National Flood Insurance Program as set forth in the Flood Disaster Protection Act of 1973, as amended by the National Flood Insurance Reform Act of 1994 (42 U.S.C. 4104b).

If the subject property is located in a Special Flood Hazard Area (SFHA), the lender is required to notify the borrower that federal flood insurance may be available and, in certain cases, required as a condition of the loan. The mandatory purchase requirement applies to loans made by federally regulated lenders for properties located in an SFHA that participates in the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP).
